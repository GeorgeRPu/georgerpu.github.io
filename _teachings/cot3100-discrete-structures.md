## Course Overview

This is the required discrete mathematics course for computer science and computer engineering majors at the University of Florida. It covers:

- Propositional logic, predicate calculus, and rules of inference
- Sets, functions, sequences, and summations
- Algorithms, growth of functions, and complexity
- Number theory: divisibility, primes, and number systems
- Induction and recursion
- Counting, permutations, and combinations
- Discrete probability
- Graphs

The Fall 2020 offering was delivered online because of the pandemic.

## Prerequisites

- MAC2311 or MAC3472 (calculus) and COP3502C or equivalent, each with a minimum grade of C
- Corequisite: COP3503 or COP3504

## Textbook

- _Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications_, Kenneth Rosen
